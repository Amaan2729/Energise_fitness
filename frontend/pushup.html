Push-Up Master Guide

Learn proper form, variations, and build upper body strength with this comprehensive tutorial.

🟢 Beginner Push-Ups

For those starting their fitness journey

  • Wall Push-Ups: Stand arm's length away from a wall, place hands flat against it, and push yourself back to standing position. 3 sets of 15 reps.
  • Incline Push-Ups: Hands on a bench or elevated surface, body at an angle. Easier than ground push-ups. 3 sets of 12-15 reps.
  • Knee Push-Ups: Start in a plank position on your knees. Lower chest to the ground and push back up. 3 sets of 10-15 reps.
  • Rest: 60-90 seconds between sets

🟡 Intermediate Push-Ups

Build strength and endurance

  • Standard Push-Ups: Full plank position, lower chest to near-ground level. 4 sets of 12-15 reps.
  • Wide-Grip Push-Ups: Hands wider than shoulders, emphasizes chest. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.
  • Diamond Push-Ups: Hands form a diamond shape, targets triceps more. 3 sets of 8-12 reps.
  • Rest: 45-60 seconds between sets

🔴 Advanced Push-Up Variations

Challenge yourself with expert-level variations

  • Archer Push-Ups: One arm extended, one arm bent. Builds unilateral strength. 3 sets of 8-10 reps per side.
  • Clapping Push-Ups: Explosive movement, clap at the top. 3 sets of 6-8 reps.
  • One-Arm Push-Ups: Ultimate upper body strength. 3 sets of 5-8 reps per side.
  • Decline Push-Ups: Feet elevated on bench, harder than standard. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.
  • Rest: 30-45 seconds between sets

📖 How to Do a Proper Push-Up

  1. Start in a plank position: Place your hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart on the floor, with your shoulders directly above your wrists. Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
  2. Engage your core: Tighten your abdominal muscles to prevent your hips from sagging. This protects your lower back and improves stability.
  3. Lower your body: Bend your elbows and slowly lower your chest towards the floor, keeping your body straight and your core engaged. Lower as far as comfortable without touching the ground.
  4. Push back up: Press through your palms to straighten your arms and return to the starting position. Keep your movements controlled and avoid locking your elbows at the top.
  5. Breathe properly: Inhale as you lower down, exhale as you push back up.
  6. Repeat: Perform the desired number of repetitions, maintaining good form throughout.

Tips for Perfecting Your Push-Up

  • Keep your body in a straight line throughout the movement—no sagging hips or piked posterior.
  • Avoid letting your elbows flare out too wide; keep them at a 45-degree angle.
  • Engage your core to protect your lower back and maximize effectiveness.
  • If you're new to push-ups, start with modified versions on your knees or against a wall.
  • Gradually increase the number of repetitions and difficulty as you build strength.
  • Focus on controlled movements rather than speed.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Sagging hips: Keeps your body rigid like a plank, not a bridge.
  • Flared elbows: Can cause shoulder strain; keep elbows at 45-degree angle.
  • Limited range: Go as low as comfortable to maximize muscle engagement.
  • Rushed reps: Slow, controlled movements are more effective than fast ones.
  • Skipping warm-up: Always warm up before intense training to prevent injury.
  • Ignoring pain: Stop if you feel sharp pain; soreness is normal, pain is not.
