Burpees Master Guide

Master this ultimate full-body exercise to build strength, endurance, and explosive power.

🟢 Beginner Burpees

For those starting their burpee journey

  • Step-Back Burpees: Instead of jumping back, step your feet back one at a time. 3 sets of 8-10 reps with 90 seconds rest.
  • No Push-Up Burpees: Skip the push-up portion. 3 sets of 10-12 reps with 75 seconds rest.
  • Modified Burpees (Incline): Place hands on elevated surface like a bench. 3 sets of 10 reps with 90 seconds rest.
  • Burpees Without Jump: End in standing position instead of jumping. 3 sets of 10 reps with 75 seconds rest.
  • Rest: 75-90 seconds between sets

🟡 Intermediate Burpees

Build strength and explosive power

  • Standard Burpees: Full burpee with push-up and jump. 4 sets of 10-15 reps with 60 seconds rest.
  • Burpee with Chest-to-Deck: Lower chest all the way to the floor on the push-up. 3 sets of 8-12 reps with 60 seconds rest.
  • Fast-Paced Burpees: Increase speed and maintain good form. 4 sets of 12-15 reps with 45 seconds rest.
  • Burpee Intervals: 30 seconds on, 30 seconds rest. 5-6 rounds.
  • Rest: 45-60 seconds between sets

🔴 Advanced Burpee Variations

Challenge yourself with expert-level variations

  • Burpee with Push-Up Clap: Clap hands at top of push-up for explosive power. 3 sets of 8-10 reps.
  • Burpee Box Jump: Jump onto a box at the end instead of normal jump. 3 sets of 8-12 reps.
  • Weighted Burpees: Wear a weighted vest while performing burpees. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.
  • Burpee with Pull-Up: End with a pull-up from a bar. 3 sets of 5-8 reps.
  • Tabata Burpees: 20 seconds all-out effort, 10 seconds rest. 8 rounds (4 minutes total).
  • Rest: 30-45 seconds between sets

📖 How to Do a Proper Burpee

  1. Start standing: Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your arms at your sides. Keep your chest up and posture straight.
  2. Squat down: Bend your knees and place your hands on the floor in front of you, shoulder-width apart. Your hands should be directly under your shoulders.
  3. Engage your core: Tighten your core muscles to protect your lower back during the movement.
  4. Kick back: Jump your feet back explosively to a plank position, keeping your body in a straight line from head to heels. Your shoulders should be directly above your wrists.
  5. Perform a push-up: Lower your chest to the floor, then press back up to the plank position. Keep your elbows close to your body.
  6. Jump feet forward: Jump your feet back towards your hands, landing in a squat position. This should be one explosive movement.
  7. Jump up: Explosively jump up, reaching your arms overhead and extending fully. Land softly and immediately go into the next rep.

Tips for Perfecting Your Burpee

  • Maintain a steady pace and control your movements throughout the exercise.
  • Keep your core engaged at all times to protect your lower back.
  • Land softly on your feet when jumping to reduce impact on your joints.
  • Ensure your hands, shoulders, and hips are properly aligned during the plank position.
  • For beginners, start with modified versions such as step-back burpees or no push-up burpees.
  • Focus on form and technique to maximize effectiveness and prevent injuries.
  • Breathe continuously throughout—exhale during exertion, inhale during recovery.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Poor plank position: Keep hips level, not sagging or piked upward.
  • Improper hand placement: Hands should be directly under shoulders, not in front.
  • Unsafe jumps: Don't land with locked knees; land softly with bent knees.
  • Weak core engagement: Always keep your core tight to protect your back.
  • Rushing the movement: Maintain control throughout each repetition.
  • Skipping the push-up: Include the full push-up for maximum upper body engagement.
  • Ignoring pain: Stop immediately if you feel sharp pain; muscle fatigue is normal, pain is not.

Pro Tips for Maximum Results

  • Warm-up properly: Do 5-10 minutes of light cardio before starting.
  • Consistency: Perform burpees 2-3 times per week with rest days in between.
  • Progressive overload: Gradually increase reps, sets, or intensity over time.
  • Proper nutrition: Fuel your body with adequate protein and carbs before and after.
  • Track progress: Keep a log of your burpee sets and reps to monitor improvement.
  • Recovery matters: Allow 48 hours between intense burpee sessions.
  • Cool down: Always stretch after your workout to aid recovery.
  • Stay hydrated: Drink water before, during, and after your workout.
